About

I build products, and the proof they work

I'm a Product Growth Analyst at Sea Group, on ShopeePay and SPaylater. I build credit products for e-commerce sellers - what gets offered, to whom, at what price, and how we'd know afterwards whether it was worth building.

Before that, Strategic Growth Specialist at Home Credit Vietnam, and a tech consultant on the SAP track at ABeam. On the side I build my own analytics projects - clustering, regression, cohort dashboards - because I'd rather build the dashboard than wait for one.

Away from the job I play guitar, hike when a weekend allows, and still spend time with the AIESEC community I helped build. All three reward showing up repeatedly, which is probably the thing I'm actually good at.
