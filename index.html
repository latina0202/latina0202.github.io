Where I'm going

Data science for healthcare

I want to work as a data scientist in health tech. In Quảng Ngãi, as in much of rural Vietnam, hospitals often lack specialists and equipment, so patients travel hours for care. Data cannot put a specialist in every district. It can help a hospital predict demand, plan staff and medicines, and follow up with patients before they need to travel.

My first step was small: a Python tool that reads booking messages at my father's clinic and logs appointments and follow-up dates. It now holds records for more than 1,000 patients.

Questions I keep coming back to

  1. How can a hospital with limited, incomplete records use models built for hospitals with much better data?
  2. How can a model handle uncertain patient demand when staff and beds are scarce?
