About

What the numbers reveal about people

I am a Product Growth Analyst at Sea Group, working on SPaylater, the buy-now-pay-later product inside ShopeePay. I grow its merchant network and lead a new cash loan for online sellers.

Before that, I spent two years at Home Credit Vietnam building the app's digital sales and launching a cash loan inside MoMo, and I trained as an SAP tech consultant at ABeam.

I studied business, not computer science, and built my data skills through practice: SQL and Python at work, small models on my own. In every role, the work I enjoyed most was the same: understanding what the numbers reveal about people, and using that to decide what to do next.

2026 - now
Product Growth Analyst
Sea Group, ShopeePay & SPaylater (BNPL)
Growth for SPaylater, its merchant network, and a new cash loan for sellers.
  • I grew the SPaylater merchant network through VNPayQR and VietQR and helped revamp the product. Disbursement volume grew 50% quarter on quarter.
  • For a new seller cash loan, I used SQL and Power BI to find which sellers were ready for credit. Volume reached about $2M a month.
  • Many approved sellers never took the money. A time-to-disburse cohort heatmap across 115,000 sellers showed where they dropped off, and that point became our follow-up trigger.
  • When pricing moved from a flat 35% rate to 25-35%, I worried sellers would be confused more than the price itself. I wrote four notifications to explain it before the change, for about 1,500 sellers.
