About

Where the money goes, and how we know it worked

I'm a Product Growth Analyst at Sea Group, working on ShopeePay and SPaylater. Most of my job is deciding where credit and incentive money should go — which sellers see which offer, at what price, and how we'd know afterwards whether it was the right call.

Before Sea Group I was a Strategic Growth Specialist at Home Credit Vietnam, and started out as a tech consultant on the SAP track at ABeam Consulting. Alongside the product work I build my own analytics projects — clustering, regression modelling, cohort dashboards — partly to keep the technical side sharp, and partly because I'd rather build the dashboard than wait for one.
